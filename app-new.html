Help & About

A quick guide to the app — what everything does and how your data is kept. For the full walkthrough, check the manual that came with your download.
Your data & privacy
Everything you enter is saved only on this device, in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to the internet, and there's no account or sign-in. This also means: if you clear your browser data, uninstall, or lose this device, your data goes with it — so back up regularly under More → Data & Backup → Export backup.

Good to know
• Upcoming only shows what's due within 3 days (or overdue) — that's intentional, not a bug.
• Transfers between your own wallets never count as income or expense.
• Currency conversions are locked in at the moment of payment, so old records never silently change when rates update.
• Editing a record always undoes the old effect first, then applies the new one — your balances stay correct.
• You can attach a receipt photo to a transaction or card purchase by tapping "📷 Attach photo" in its form.
• Turn on App Lock (More → App Lock) to require a PIN before the app opens.
